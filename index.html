        Evidence has a boundary

        The executor reads evidence through Zetaris under a scoped role. Recorded refusals show that a later time wall, raw tables and self-granted access are blocked.

        What ran behind the interface

        1. Scout IntentRoutes the request and verifies a signed tool contract.
        2. Zetaris → H-MEMFetches walled evidence and recalls the package’s current stances.
        3. Two Nemotron pathsCompare recent raw history with organized package memory.
        4. Planner ⇄ Evaluator → VerifierRequests more evidence, checks cited rows, or escalates to the lead.
        5. Human confirmationRequired before a proposal is committed.

        H-MEM and World Model also keep commitments, notes and watch events. The SQL view and judge decide whether to reopen; the engines keep the record.

          About this replay

          Cases, model responses, SQL results and receipts are recordings. Opening a trace reads the bundled data; it does not call a model, run SQL, grant access, commit a decision or reopen a CVE.

          Source URLs, account identifiers and unrelated organization names are visibly redacted. CVEs, decision labels, evidence IDs, dates and measured results are preserved.
